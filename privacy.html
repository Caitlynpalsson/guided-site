Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Guided (“Guided,” “we,” “us”), operated by Caitlyn Palsson, handles information when you use the Guided app on iOS and on the web (together, the “App”). We built Guided to stay quiet: we collect as little as possible, and most of what you enter never leaves your device.

1. Information stored on your device

Guided does not require an account. The information you enter — such as your name, the goals and aims you name, your vision text, the people and subjects you pray for, your season and what is on your heart, your chosen voice, saved meditations, affirmations, and prayers — is stored locally on your device (in app storage on iOS, or in your browser’s local storage on the web). We do not operate a database of your entries and we cannot see them.

2. Sensitive information

Prayers, religious beliefs, and the personal goals you share may reveal sensitive information, including your religious or philosophical beliefs and, potentially, information about your health or the health of others. We treat this information as sensitive. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles about you. It is only sent off your device in the limited ways described below, and only with your consent where required.

3. AI features (sent to xAI only with your consent)

Guided can generate personalized meditations, affirmations, and prayers using artificial intelligence. If you consent to use these AI features, the App sends the information needed to generate the content — your name, your goals, your vision text, your prayer subjects (the people and things you pray for), and related context such as your current season and the Scripture verses chosen — to xAI (X.AI LLC), our AI provider, to generate a response. The request passes through our web server only to relay it to xAI; we do not store it. xAI processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy (x.ai/legal/privacy-policy). If you do not consent, the AI features are not used and this information is not sent. You can avoid AI features at any time by not using them.

4. Voice audio

On iOS, spoken sessions are generated on your device using your iPhone’s built-in speech voices; the text and audio stay on your device. On the web, when you choose to listen to a session, the session text is sent to xAI to generate voice audio, which is returned to your browser for playback. Named voices are synthetic and are not recordings of real people.

5. Scripture lookups

When you open a full Bible passage that is not already included in the App, Guided requests the public-domain text from bible-api.com. That request contains only the verse reference (for example, “John 14:27”). We do not send your name, goals, or prayers with it. Like any website, that service may receive your IP address (on the web, the request is relayed through our server).

6. Fonts and hosting (web)

The web version of Guided loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches those fonts, Google may receive your IP address and standard browser information (such as user agent). See Google’s Privacy Policy. The iOS app bundles its fonts and does not contact Google Fonts. The web app and this website are served by hosting providers that, like all web servers, may process IP addresses and request logs to deliver and secure the service. This informational website itself does not load Google Fonts, analytics, or other third-party scripts.

7. Purchases

Subscriptions on iOS are purchased and processed by Apple through your Apple ID. Purchases on the web are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. We may receive limited information needed to confirm your subscription status (for example, whether a subscription is active, and a transaction or customer identifier). Apple’s and Stripe’s handling of your payment information is governed by their privacy policies (Apple, Stripe).

8. No tracking, no ads

Guided does not show ads, does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and does not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

9. Children

Guided is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, contact us at caitlyn.palsson@gmail.com and we will help delete it.

10. Retention and deletion

Information stored on your device stays there until you delete it. You can delete your data in the App (for example, from the You / settings screen), by clearing the App’s site data in your browser, or by deleting the iOS app. We do not keep copies of your entries. Data sent to xAI for AI features is used to generate your content and is retained by xAI according to its policies. Records held by Apple or Stripe about purchases are retained according to their policies and legal obligations. You may also email caitlyn.palsson@gmail.com to request deletion of any information we hold about you (for example, support emails).

11. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent at any time (withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened); and to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on your explicit consent to process sensitive data (such as religious beliefs) for AI features, and on our legitimate interests or the performance of a contract for purchases and support. California residents have the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights; we do not sell or share personal information and do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. Because most of your data lives only on your device, you can exercise many of these rights directly in the App. For anything else, email caitlyn.palsson@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by law and may need to verify your request.

12. International transfers

We and our service providers (such as xAI, Apple, Stripe, Google, and our hosting providers) may process information in the United States and other countries whose laws may differ from those where you live. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

13. Security

Data sent from the App is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Because your entries are stored on your device, please protect your device with a passcode and keep its software up to date.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If changes are material, we will provide additional notice, such as in the App.

15. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: caitlyn.palsson@gmail.com (Caitlyn Palsson, Guided).
