Guided is a quiet companion for prayer and guided meditation. Tell it what you are hoping for — a calling, a healing, a new season — and it shapes a spoken meditation, affirmations, and prayer around that goal, grounded in Scripture.

Guided meditations

Arrive, hear the Word, rest, pray, and receive a blessing — sized to your moment, whether walking, getting ready, or winding down for sleep.
