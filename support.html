Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription on iPhone?

Open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Guided → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of your trial or current period to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

How do I cancel on the web?

Open the subscription management link in the App (the Stripe customer portal) and choose Cancel, or email caitlyn.palsson@gmail.com and we’ll help.

How do I restore my purchases?

On iPhone, open Guided and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe. On the web, use the same email you used at checkout, or contact us if your access didn’t carry over.

How do I get a refund?

iPhone purchases are refunded by Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com. For web purchases made through Stripe, email caitlyn.palsson@gmail.com.

How do I delete my data?

Your entries live on your device. Delete them in the App from the You screen, or delete the iOS app (or clear the site data for Guided in your browser) to remove everything. We don’t keep a copy. To request deletion of anything else we might hold (like support emails), email caitlyn.palsson@gmail.com.

What is sent to AI?

Only if you consent, your name, goals, vision text, and prayer subjects are sent to xAI to generate your meditations and affirmations. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Is Guided a replacement for counseling or church?

No. Guided is a companion for prayer, not medical or mental-health care. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services or call or text 988 in the U.S.
